Real Hardware Workflow

How GPS Toller works with a GPS device installed in a vehicle

REAL-WORLD IMPLEMENTATION

In the actual deployment, a GPS device connected to the vehicle continuously provides location coordinates. The device sends the data to the server, where Django processes the coordinates, matches them against highway data and stores the processed information in the database.

01

Vehicle

The vehicle is equipped with a GPS tracking device. The device continuously determines the vehicle's geographical position.

02

GPS Device

GPS coordinates such as latitude and longitude are collected from the vehicle's location.

03

Data Transmission

The tracking device sends the location information to the backend server through its communication interface.

04

Django Server

Django receives the incoming location data and processes the vehicle coordinates.

05

Geospatial Processing

The coordinates are compared with predefined highway/geospatial data to determine whether the vehicle is travelling on a toll route.

06

Database

Vehicle location, trip information and calculated toll information are stored in the database.

07

Toll Calculation

The system calculates the travelled distance and determines the corresponding toll amount.

08

Invoice & Payment

The calculated toll is converted into an invoice. The customer can then complete the payment through the billing section.
